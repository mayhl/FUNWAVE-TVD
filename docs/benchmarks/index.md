# Benchmarks

Cost and scaling measurements: time per step, strong and weak scaling, and the
effect of decomposition, threading and solver choices on throughput.

Benchmarks follow the same page framework as Examples and Validation — a deck, a
generated setup section, and results stamped with the revision and machine they were
measured on. They differ in one respect, and it is the important one: a benchmark
reports a measurement without a verdict. There is no oracle and no pass or fail,
only a number and the machine it came from. A benchmark therefore compares against
previous measurements, never against a tolerance.
