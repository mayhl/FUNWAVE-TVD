Detailed Description

Solver-class module: unlike kernel_* modules (pure compute, no MPI), solver_* modules may hold a type_grid_2d reference and communicate. Callers assemble coefficients with a kernel, invoke a solver here, then consume the solution with another kernel (see model_kernel_etauv_mod).

Buffers may be device-resident (device-aware MPI); do not add host-side staging assumptions to the message paths.
