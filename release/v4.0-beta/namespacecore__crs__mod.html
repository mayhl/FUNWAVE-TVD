Detailed Description

Provides forward and inverse coordinate transforms between an external CRS (geographic or projected) and the model's local Cartesian metre grid.

Three modes are supported:

Mode constant Description
CRS_LOCAL Ungeoreferenced; identity transform.
CRS_PROJECTED UTM or state-plane (metres); pure translation by origin.
CRS_GEOGRAPHIC Longitude/latitude (degrees); flat-Earth approximation.

Coordinate transform

The local grid is related to the external CRS by a rotation and translation. Given external coordinates \((x_e, y_e)\), the displacement from the grid origin \((\lambda_0, \phi_0)\) is first converted to metres \((\delta x, \delta y)\) (see below), then rotated by the grid azimuth \(\theta\) (counter-clockwise from east):

\[ \begin{pmatrix} x_{\rm loc} \\ y_{\rm loc} \end{pmatrix} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}}_{R(\theta)} \begin{pmatrix} \delta x \\ \delta y \end{pmatrix} \]

The inverse is \(({\delta x}, {\delta y}) = R^T (x_{\rm loc}, y_{\rm loc})\).

Flat-Earth geographic projection

For CRS_GEOGRAPHIC, angular displacements \((\Delta\lambda, \Delta\phi)\) from the origin are converted to metres using a first-order flat-Earth (equidistant) approximation valid for domains smaller than approximately 200 km [torge2012geodesy:]

\[ \delta x = R_\oplus \cos\phi_0 \;\frac{\pi}{180}\; \Delta\lambda, \qquad \delta y = R_\oplus \;\frac{\pi}{180}\; \Delta\phi, \]

where \(R_\oplus\) is the mean Earth radius and \(\phi_0\) is the reference (origin) latitude.
